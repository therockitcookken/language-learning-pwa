# Data Plan

The import pipeline accepts licensed CSV/JSON sources, preserves source metadata, normalizes multilingual forms, validates duplicate IDs/headwords, pinyin syntax, translations, levels, topic mappings and quiz references, then emits a quality report. Data sources and licences are recorded in `data-sources.md` and `data-license.md`.

Initial seed delivery uses verified content available under permitted licences. The project records actual imported totals; 2,000 Chinese entries, 2,000 English entries, 500 factory terms per language, 250 grammar lessons per language, 3,000 quiz items and 300 dialogues remain quantitative acceptance targets for staged imports rather than fabricated data.
