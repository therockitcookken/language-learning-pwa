# Data Sources Report

## Overview

The Factory Language Learning dataset combines verified open Mandarin and English industrial vocabularies, grammar structures, SOP procedures, workplace safety manuals, and dialogues.

## Datasets Included

1. **Mandarin Factory Vocabulary & Sentences (Tiếng Trung Công Xưởng)**:
   - Simplified & Traditional Hanzi headwords
   - Tone-marked Pinyin & Numeric Pinyin
   - HSK 1-6 levels & Factory Domains (Safety, PPE, CNC, Assembly, QC, Maintenance, Warehouse, HR, Salary, 5S, Kaizen)
   - Real workplace example sentences and translations
2. **English Factory Vocabulary & IPA (Tiếng Anh Công Nghiệp)**:
   - Headwords & International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA)
   - CEFR A1-C2 levels
   - Factory Domains & Usage Notes
3. **Industrial Grammar Lessons (Ngữ Pháp Công Xưởng)**:
   - Chinese HSK Grammar & English Technical CEFR Grammar
   - Formulas, Usage, Correct vs Wrong Comparisons, and Factory Scenarios
4. **Workplace Dialogues & Scenarios (Hội Thoại Nhà Máy)**:
   - 300+ Situational dialogues for shift handovers, incident reporting, maintenance, and job interviews.
5. **Interactive Quiz Bank**:
   - 3,000+ quiz questions covering 18 question types.
